Brand Identity

cyberLab

A sealed lab and a captured trace that ends in a check mark: every pcap is generated in isolation and verified before it leaves.

Design Concept

Sealed lab, verified trace

Symbol

The outer rounded square is the lab boundary: an internal Docker network with no published ports and no route out. Inside it, the dark chamber holds one captured trace. The line starts flat, spikes where the attack or protocol traffic happens, and finishes as a check mark, because a trace only leaves the lab after tshark has verified it.

Color

cyberLab uses the MMT family palette shared with mmt-security and mmt-dpi. Golden Amber marks the trace, the one thing the skill hands back. Charcoal and taupe stay neutral so the amber is the first thing you see.

Typography

Inter Bold with slightly tight tracking. "cyber" is set in charcoal and "Lab" in amber, the same camelCase split the mmtDPI wordmark uses.
